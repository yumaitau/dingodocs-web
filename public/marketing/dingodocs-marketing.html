DingoDocs / Product brief 02

Keep every engagement in view.

Connect delivery work to the client, scope, evidence, findings, tasks, reports, and retests that give it meaning.

Scope stays attached

Approved targets, rules of engagement, assets, and methodology remain with the work they govern.

Evidence carries history

Clients see approved material

Reports keep their versions

DingoDocs / Engagement control 03

Publishing is a deliberate state change.

Findings carry structured technical detail, impact, remediation, evidence, comments, ownership, and review history.

Author with context

Keep executive summary, technical detail, reproduction, impact, remediation, and verification guidance together.

Review before release

Draft and review states remain visible. Reasoned overrides create audit records.

Separate visibility from publication

Client visibility is independent from internal approval, so delivery boundaries stay clear.

Retest without losing history

Retests preserve the original finding version while recording what changed and what was verified.

Reports built from approved work.

Assemble a controlled client deliverable after the content has earned its way through review.
